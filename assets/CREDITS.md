# Original game and audio

Gun Mayhem and Gun Mayhem 2 by Kevin Gu and their original credited teams.
This is an unofficial fan modification called Gun Mayhem 3 Online.
Gun Mayhem 3 Online project: IntelGenV2.

`gm1-menu.mp3` is the `music111` sound (DefineSound 784) from GM1.
The GM1 menu explicitly plays it from frame 10 when `gotomenu` is true.
`gm1-setup.mp3` is GM1's `music222` sound (DefineSound 785), used for its
Options and setup screens. Both tracks are extracted without re-encoding.
